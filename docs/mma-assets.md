Factual generic titles are used; no job location, date, duration, price or customer identity is invented. The images are presented as company-sourced work examples, not AI-generated work.

Original image bytes are preserved in `public/assets/mma/originals/`. Web copies are JPEGs at quality 86, resized only to fit within 1600×1600 without enlargement or cropping. No objects, garden conditions, colours or camera watermarks were changed. Preserve the visible Galaxy A04s marks and the image composition.
